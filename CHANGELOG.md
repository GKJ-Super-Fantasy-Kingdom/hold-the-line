# Changelog

## 0.2.10

- Restore wager and relic reward persistence on Super Fantasy Kingdom 0.8.070 while retaining the 0.8.069 save hook.
- Update the package icon and screenshots for the current wager cards and relic picker.

## 0.2.9

- Wager cards, stake arrows and chooser footers show a white edge on hover, focus and press, and clear it when the interaction ends.

## 0.2.8

- Blank clicks leave the wager and earned relic choosers open.
- Pass and Claim Later use the game's outlined cancel button style.

## 0.2.7

- Adjust the Coin Wager with the native overlay shortcuts. The arrows show the current controller or keyboard binding.

## 0.2.6

- Wager selection now places its cards over the darkened kingdom, with the rules above the card row and Pass below it.

## 0.2.5

- Wager cards now highlight on mouse hover and controller focus. Unavailable cards dim their artwork and show the missing coins or eligible relics.

## 0.2.4

Clarify the mod’s description and compatibility for players.

## 0.2.3

- Choose an adjustable Coin Wager, a fixed 20-coin Relic Wager, or Pass before battle. Both cards show the cost before acceptance.
- Set your coin stake with the arrows. For example, staking 6 coins returns 9 on a flawless night, for a net profit of 3.
- Win a Relic Wager to choose one of three eligible relics in the native chest picker. Claim Later keeps those same choices across Continue.
- The two portrait wager cards feature larger illustrations, a bronze frame and wood grain for coins, and silver trim and woven diamonds for relics.
- Accepted wagers from 0.1.5 retain their original terms. A damaging hit still forfeits the wager even if the wall is repaired before dawn.

## 0.1.5

Updated the package icon to show the Castle Wall and the gold wager coin.

## 0.1.4

First public release. Stake 1 coin before battle and receive 3 coins total for a clean night. A damaging hit loses the stake even after a repair. The two wager buttons sit directly below the coin summary at both UI scales.

## 0.1.3

Center the wager title on the native ribbon at both UI scales.

## 0.1.2

Show the entry fee and total payout on a native coin row. Add a Back prompt and a native dialogue heading. Update affordability while the card is open and report the result before the next offer. Restore an unanswered wager from a pre-battle checkpoint.

## 0.1.1

Resolve the active controller's submit glyph on the wager offer.

## 0.1.0

Adds an optional Castle Wall wager, native offer and controller controls, damage tracking, and checkpointed settlement.
